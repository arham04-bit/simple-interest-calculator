# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone participating in this project. All contributors are expected to communicate respectfully and contribute positively.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Being respectful and considerate of others.
* Accepting constructive feedback.
* Focusing on what is best for the project and community.
* Using welcoming and inclusive language.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, inappropriate comments, and any conduct that makes others feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior. They may take appropriate and fair action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related activities.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and investigated fairly and confidentially whenever possible. Appropriate action may be taken based on the circumstances.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take actions including a warning, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restriction of participation, or permanent removal from the project when necessary.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and is provided to promote a respectful and inclusive open-source community.
